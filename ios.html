When requesting help, include the app version, iPhone/Watch model and a description of the issue. Avoid sending health readings, route coordinates or account credentials unless genuinely needed.

Watch workouts and syncing

In supported testing builds, link the Watch workout before starting if you want to send it to the iPhone account. Finish, tap Done, then Send next to iPhone while Veyrazo is open and signed in to the original account. Received records appear under Workout history → Watch workout history.

This Watch history is local-only; it does not yet update cloud storage, My Plan, daily challenges or progress totals. Missing readings are shown as unavailable rather than estimated. Health permissions and real-device availability can affect readings.

Keep Android and iOS guidance separate

Android uses its own onboarding and integrations. Apple Health, Apple Watch, Sign in with Apple and the iOS settings paths on these pages do not describe Android. The existing Android policy and deletion pages remain unchanged.

Questions: support@slothbloom.com. Do not send passwords, verification codes or unnecessary health information.
